# Incident Report Template

## Incident ID
Use the AegisTrace incident ID.

## Title
Short analyst-confirmed incident title.

## Severity
Low / Medium / High / Critical

## Status
Open / Investigating / Contained / Closed

## Summary
Explain what happened and why the finding was promoted from detection/IOC to an incident.

## Linked Evidence
- Detection ID / rule:
- IOC ID / value:
- Host:
- User:
- Source IP:

## Timeline
Record relevant event times and analyst actions.

## Analysis
Describe the evidence reviewed, alternative explanations and final assessment.

## Response
Document recommended or completed response actions. Do not claim firewall/endpoint changes unless they were actually performed outside AegisTrace.

## Conclusion
State the final analyst conclusion and remaining follow-up.
