# IOC Investigation and Containment

**Mapping:** IOC Triage

## Steps

1. Identify IOC type and source event.
2. Review host and user context.
3. Search telemetry for additional occurrences.
4. Review related network/process activity.
5. Determine expected, unknown or suspicious status.
6. Record a proposed blocklist entry only after analyst review.
7. Document evidence and escalate when justified.

> Guidance only. AegisTrace does not automatically change endpoint or firewall configuration.
